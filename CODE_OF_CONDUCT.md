# Code of Conduct

MailChat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and don't harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that don't follow this.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the email on their GitHub profile, or through a private [security advisory](https://github.com/master-eugene/mailchat/security/advisories/new) if it's sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.
